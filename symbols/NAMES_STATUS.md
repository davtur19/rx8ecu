# NAMES_STATUS — catalogo simboli per ROM

This file states the naming status of the function names for the 9 public stock ROMs.
It was written after the "CATALOGAZIONE" campaign (IDA-ai fusion into the canonical
+ cross-ROM through content signature).

Columns: **totali** (functions in the catalog), **descrittivi** (name not FUN_/sub_/loc_/nullsub_), **anonimi** (generic name), **fonte** (source column of the CSV), **delta anon vs recce**.

> Reliability note: the catalogs `60E0FC00*` are generated by Ghidra/equinox analysis (reliable function boundaries). The catalogs of the other ROMs derive with
> `tools/xmap_names.py --derive` (entry discovery from `bsr`+pooled pointer, range
> `0x40..0x6CE00`) and therefore are **over-segmented** (~2× the canonical). The
> transferred `ghidra-hand-xmap` names are however 1:1 matches at high confidence.
> The *descrittivi count* is reliable. The *total* is provisional until we do a
> function analysis with Ghidra.

## Delta canonico vs recce precedente
