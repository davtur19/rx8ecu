## WHY IT DOES NOT MATCH (structural divergences)

1) add16bitSaturate / addSaturate8Bit:
   - ROM: `mov.l @(pc),r5` with ONE literal 0xFFFF/0x00FF, `cmp/hs`/`cmp/ge`,
     `bf/s` with rts delay-slot `mov r4,r0`; clamp with `mov r5,r4` and reuse
     register r4.
   - GCC 14: turns `sum >= 0xFFFF` into `sum > 0xFFFE`: it loads 0xFFFE,
     uses `cmp/hi`, `bt`, has TWO literal pools (0xFFFE and 0xFFFF), two
     separate rts and puts `extu.w r2,r0` in the delay slot. The result
     register differs (r2).
2) addS32Saturate:
   - ROM: idiom `addv r4,r5` + `cmp/pz` + `addc` (signed-overflow detect).
   - GCC 14 NEVER emits `addv` for pure C: the 64-bit C produces the
     `subc`+`addc` (64-bit) sequence, `__builtin_add_overflow` produces
     `add`+`xor`+`not` ("modern" overflow detect). You need
     inline-asm/intrinsic.
3) seed_mixer: the ROM is low-optimization codegen (store/reload of bytes
   on the stack, PC-relative pool); GCC 14 -O0/-O1/-O2/-Os produces
   different structures. No match even at -O0 (different layout/reg alloc).

## WHY THE SOURCE add16bitSaturate_reg.c WINS (AND WHAT MAKES THE OTHERS LOSE)

  - `max` as a VARIABLE (register unsigned max __asm__("r5") = 0xFFFF):
    GCC 14 and GCC 3.4.6 fold `sum >= 0xFFFF` into `sum > 0xFFFE`
    (cmp/hi + 2 literals), while the ROM loads ONE 0xFFFF and does cmp/hs.
  - register pins `__asm__("r4")/"r5"`: the ROM keeps the sum in r4 and
    the constant in r5 (clamp `mov r5,r4`); without the pins gcc chooses
    r0/r1 (62.5%).

    puts `extu.w r4,r0` in the delay slot (last different instruction, 79.2%).
  - -O1 (not -O2/-Os: 79.2%) and -fomit-frame-pointer (without: prologue+frame).

## WHY IT DOES NOT MATCH (divergence patterns, 4 families)

  1) Selectors ?:  (atu/mbox/getHCAN): ROM `bt(s)`+`bra` (constant 0x0200
     loaded DIRECTLY into r4: `mov.w @(pc),r4; add r5,r4`, and `mov r5,r4`
     in the `bra` delay slot); gcc 3.4.6 inverts to `bf.s` with `mov r5,r4`
     in the delay and loads the constant into r1 (`mov.w @(pc),r1; mov r5,r4;
     add r1,r4`). First divergence +0x06 (branch opcode).
  2) Return register (complement_shift_u16/encode): ROM accumulates in r4
     and does `rts; mov r4,r0`; gcc 3.4.6 materializes in r0 (`mov r3,r0;
     rts; add r2,r0`). The pin `__asm__("r4")` is not enough (gcc folds
     the add onto the return reg.).
  3) Booleans (charging_status/obd_67154): ROM builds 0/1 with branches
     (`mov #0,r4`/`mov #1,r4`); gcc 3.4.6 uses `movt` (`tst`+`movt`,
     `and #31,r0`+`movt`). Structural divergence, no flag changes it.
  4) Shift/loop (shift_right_8_r0): ROM is 8x `shar r0` unrolled; gcc emits
     a loop or `shar` with count. 5.6%, far.

## DEFINITIVE LIMITS (structural divergences, cannot be bypassed)

  1) addv / special idioms (addS32Saturate@0x2304): no pure C or flag of
     any release (3.4.6/3.3.6/3.2.3) emits addv -> assembly-first.
  2) Branch polarity in the selectors (bt+bra vs bf.s): gcc always
     normalizes bf.s. Only a C rewrite in single cases (pulse) or
     m1.noifconv (atu_spec) can change it. Structural.
  3) tst #imm (0xC8): pattern in sh.md 3.2.3/3.3.6 but the combiner does
     not fold; absent in 3.4.6. No release ever emits it.
  4) movt booleans: ONLY -fno-if-conversion can bypass it (3.3.6+/3.4.6).
     It does not exist on 3.2.3.
  5) SECTION-level literal pool layout (pool interleaved after the
     prologue of the adjacent function, for example atu@0x1FB8):
     cause of the 95% instead of 100%. -mrelax/-mhitachi/-mspace do NOT
     change it. No single-file flag can reach it (test
     sweep_relax_gcc346.py, REPORT §15.2).
  6) Automatic widen of the sub-word parameter (3.3.6/3.2.3): it kills
     the _match recipes (double extension) -> because the MATCH recipe
     is BOUND to 3.4.6.
